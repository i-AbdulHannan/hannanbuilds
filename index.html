ABOUT

The story behind
the code.

I taught myself to code in 2022. No CS degree, no bootcamp promising a six-figure job in twelve weeks — just a laptop and a problem worth solving. By 2023, I'd turned that into an actual career: Aptech, a Saylani training program, and freelance PHP work that never stopped. Since going professional, I've shipped over 100 projects and picked up 7 hackathon wins along the way.

Today I run VisionBuild, a software agency based in Karachi. We build for the businesses everyone else skips past — small clinics, local SMBs, and founders who need a working product instead of another meeting. No framework for the sake of a framework, no infrastructure bill nobody asked for — I build in PHP, MySQL, Tailwind, and vanilla JavaScript, because the businesses I build for run on shared hosting, not venture capital.

Right now, most of my time goes into workroom — a client transparency and project management tool I'm building because I was tired of running client updates over WhatsApp threads that disappear the moment someone scrolls.
